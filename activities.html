School Activities

At Green Valley School, we offer a wide range of activities that help students develop skills beyond academics. Our programs encourage creativity, teamwork, leadership, and personal growth.



🏆 Sports Programs

Football

Competitive football teams for all age groups with regular training and inter-school matches.

Basketball

Indoor basketball court with coached teams and participation in regional tournaments.

Track & Field

Running, jumping, and throwing events that build speed, strength, and endurance.

🏸

Tennis

Individual and doubles tennis coaching with proper courts and equipment.

🏏

Cricket

Popular cricket program with batting, bowling, and fielding training for all students.

🧘

Yoga & Fitness

Mindfulness and physical fitness classes promoting health and well-being.
